---

In `lambeq`, the conversion of a sentence into a [quantum circuit](/lambeq/glossary/#term-quantum-circuit) goes through the steps shown in [Fig. 1](/lambeq/guide/pipeline/#fig-pipeline).

<a id="fig-pipeline" />
<figure><img src="/lambeq-assets/_static/images/pipeline.png" alt="../_static/images/pipeline.png" /><figcaption><strong>Fig. 1</strong> The general pipeline.</figcaption></figure>

In more detail:

1.  A [syntax tree](/lambeq/glossary/#term-syntax-tree) for the sentence is obtained by calling a statistical [CCG parser](/lambeq/guide/parsing/). `lambeq` is equipped with a detailed API that greatly simplifies this process, and ships with support for several widely used parsers.
2.  Internally, the [parse tree](/lambeq/glossary/#term-syntax-tree) is converted into a [string diagram](/lambeq/guide/string-diagrams/). This is an abstract representation of the sentence reflecting the relationships between the words as defined by the [compositional model](/lambeq/glossary/#term-compositional-model) of choice, independently of any implementation decisions that take place at a lower level.
3.  The [string diagram](/lambeq/glossary/#term-string-diagram) can be simplified or otherwise transformed by the application of [rewriting rules](/lambeq/tutorials/rewrite/); these can be used for example to remove specific interactions between words that might be considered redundant for the task at hand, or in order to make the computation more amenable to implementation on a quantum processing unit.
4.  The resulting [string diagram](/lambeq/glossary/#term-string-diagram) can be converted into a concrete [quantum circuit](/lambeq/glossary/#term-quantum-circuit) (or a [tensor network](/lambeq/glossary/#term-tensor-network) in the case of a \"classical\" experiment), based on a specific [parameterisation](/lambeq/tutorials/parameterise/) scheme and concrete choices of [ansätze](/lambeq/glossary/#term-ansatz-plural-ans-tze). `lambeq` features an extensible class hierarchy containing a selection of pre-defined [ansätze](/lambeq/glossary/#term-ansatz-plural-ans-tze), appropriate for both classical and quantum experiments.
5.  Now the output of the pipeline ([quantum circuit](/lambeq/glossary/#term-quantum-circuit) or [tensor network](/lambeq/glossary/#term-tensor-network)) is ready to be used for [training](/lambeq/training/). Since Release [Release notes](/lambeq/release-notes/), `lambeq` provides a detailed hierarchy of model and trainer classes that cover many of the important use-cases of supervised learning.

In the case of a fully quantum pipeline, the trainer will first process the [quantum circuit](/lambeq/glossary/#term-quantum-circuit) by calling a quantum compiler, and then it will upload the result onto a quantum computer, while in the classical case the [tensor network](/lambeq/glossary/#term-tensor-network) will be passed to an ML or optimisation library, such as [PyTorch](/lambeq/glossary/#term-pytorch) or JAX.
