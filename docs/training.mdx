The following examples demonstrate the usage of the [`training`](/lambeq/api/lambeq.training#lambeq.training) package for classical and quantum training scenarios.

- [Classical case](/lambeq/tutorials/trainer-classical/)

  Convert [string diagrams](/lambeq/glossary/#term-string-diagram) into tensor networks and train them with [PyTorch](/lambeq/glossary/#term-pytorch).

- [Quantum case](/lambeq/tutorials/trainer-quantum/)

  Create [quantum circuits](/lambeq/glossary/#term-quantum-circuit) using the [tket](/lambeq/glossary/#term-tket) backend and train them with [`QuantumTrainer`](/lambeq/api/lambeq.training#lambeq.training.QuantumTrainer).

- [Hybrid case](/lambeq/tutorials/trainer-hybrid/)

  See how to utilise the powerful [PennyLane](/lambeq/glossary/#term-pennylane) backend to train pure and hybrid quantum models.

- [DisCoCirc training](/lambeq/tutorials/discocirc-mc-task/)

  Convert entire paragraphs or documents into [DisCoCirc](/lambeq/glossary/#term-discocirc) circuits and train them with `lambeq`\'s [`PennyLaneModel`](/lambeq/api/lambeq.training#lambeq.training.PennyLaneModel).

- [Manual pipeline](/lambeq/manual-training/)

  Learn how to create custom training loops for your `lambeq` models.
