---
title: "From sentence to circuit"
---

`lambeq` offers great flexibility when converting your texts into circuits, allowing you to customise and fine-tune each step in the process by providing a number of [compositional models](/lambeq/glossary/#term-compositional-model), [rewrite rules](/lambeq/glossary/#term-rewrite-rule) and parameterisation schemes known as [ansätze](/lambeq/glossary/#term-ansatz-plural-ans-tze). This section explains all the necessary details.
