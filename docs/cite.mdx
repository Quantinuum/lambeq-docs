---
title: "How to cite"
---

If you use `lambeq` for your research, please cite the accompanying paper ([Kartsaklis et al., 2021](/lambeq/bibliography/#bib-kartsaklis_2021)):

``` bash
@article{kartsaklis2021lambeq,
   title={lambeq: {A}n {E}fficient {H}igh-{L}evel {P}ython {L}ibrary for {Q}uantum {NLP}},
   author={Dimitri Kartsaklis
           and Ian Fan
           and Richie Yeung
           and Anna Pearson
           and Robin Lorenz
           and Alexis Toumi
           and Giovanni de Felice
           and Konstantinos Meichanetzidis
           and Stephen Clark
           and Bob Coecke},
   year={2021},
   journal={arXiv preprint arXiv:2110.04236},
}
```

The `DisCoCirc` extension introduced in Release [Release notes](/lambeq/release-notes/) is described in detail in this paper ([Krawchuk et al., 2025](/lambeq/bibliography/#bib-krawchuk_2025)):

``` bash
@article{krawchuk2025,
   title={Efficient {G}eneration of {P}arameterised {Q}uantum {C}ircuits from {L}arge {T}exts},
   author={Colin Krawchuk
           and Nikhil Khatri
           and Neil John Ortega
           and Dimitri Kartsaklis},
   year={2025},
   journal={arXiv preprint arXiv:2505.13208},
}
```
